About this card. This app is an unofficial launcher built for quick, one-tap access — it is not produced by or affiliated with NIOS. Cards open the genuine nios.ac.in / sdmis.nios.ac.in pages inside this app, loaded directly from NIOS's own servers with nothing copied or intercepted. Login-sensitive pages may still refuse to display inside any outside app (their own anti-clickjacking protection) — if that happens you'll see a one-tap button to continue in your browser instead. Always check the domain shown at the top of the screen before entering your enrollment number or password.
